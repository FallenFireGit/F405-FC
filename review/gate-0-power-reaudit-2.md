# Gate 0 re-audit 2 (third round): F405-FC power design

Auditor: fresh, independent Gate 0 power re-auditor, 2026-10-11. I did not write either earlier report. I did not modify any
other file and did not commit.

**Audited:** `design/power.md` (whole file, with a focus on 0, 2.1-2.2, 3.4, 4, 7-9, 12, 13 and 14), `design/decisions.md` (D-03, D-07,
D-11, D-12, D-14, D-16), the power parts of `design/circuit.py` (lines 44-74 and 203-278, plus the 3V3 decoupling, J3/R41, D4 and
D7-D9 at lines 296-461), the committed netlist `kicad/F405FC.net` (power nets only), the U4 and 3V3 cap positions in
`kicad/F405FC.kicad_pcb`, and `gen/power_calc_lmr.py`. The netlist matches circuit.py for every refdes I checked (C1-C24, C29, R41,
D4/D5/D7/D8/D9/D10).

**Method:** I extracted `lp38692.pdf`, `lp5907.pdf`, `lmr38020.pdf` and `JST_SH_eSH.pdf` with `pdftotext -layout` into the scratchpad.
`JST_SH_eSH.pdf` is image-only, so no text came out. Nothing from `datasheets/` was executed. I parsed the netlist and PCB with my own
`python3 -I` snippets and recomputed the thermal arithmetic with `scratchpad/calc/r3.py`. That script uses the same loss model as
`gen/power_calc_lmr.py` (RDS hot ×1.45, 10 ns edges, 30 mW drive, IRMS²·DCR + 50 mW core), adds a dropout branch, and charges the
3V3 domain as 4.76 V × I(3V3) (LDO drop plus the loads). Anything I could not check against a document on file is marked
**unverified**.
