# Gate 0 audit: F405-FC power design (rev B)

Auditor: independent Gate 0 power review, 2026-10-11
Audited: `design/power.md` (rev B), `gen/power_calc_lmr.py`. Checked against `design/requirements.md`, `design/decisions.md`
(accepted deviations D-03, D-04, D-06) and `design/pinmap.md` rev C sections 6-7.
I did not modify the author's files. Every number marked "recomputed" comes from my own script
(`scratchpad/calc/audit.py`, not committed). It was run with `python3 -I`, and nothing from `datasheets/` was executed.

## Findings

| ID | Sev | Finding | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0-01 | **MAJOR** | **The thermal analysis does not support the "worst-case Tj 104 C" claim used to justify D-03.** (a) It applies a single-IC JEDEC RθJA (42.9 C/W, a 76 × 114 mm 2s2p board) to each IC separately, so the two ICs on a 36 × 36 mm board never heat each other. The doc's own board total is about 3.3 W. Board-level still-air estimate: 2 faces × 13 cm² × h 12-18 W/m²K (convection + radiation) gives **21-32 C/W board-to-ambient, so ΔT_board ≈ 70-106 C at 3.3 W**. Then Tj(U1) ≈ 50 + 70…106 + ψJB 13.8 × 1.26 ≈ **137-173 C (TSD 163 C)**, and the board itself reaches about 120-156 C. That exceeds the STM32F405RG**T6** 85 C ambient grade, the flash/IMU ratings and the LP38692's 125 C operating Tj. (b) The U2 **dropout loss on 2S/3S is omitted**: 1.5² × 0.303 × 1.45 ≈ 0.97 W (12.2 lists only 4S/6S for 10 V). (c) Switching loss assumes tr + tf = 10 ns total with no Coss, dead-time or Qrr term. For an 80 V-class integrated stage switching 25-42 V, 20 ns total is equally plausible: +0.17 W (6S) / +0.29 W (42 V) for U1. Coss at 100-200 pF adds 0.05-0.10 W. The RDS hot factor of 1.45 is reasonable for a typical Tj of 110-125 C (≈ 0.45 %/C), but it is applied to **typical** RDS (no max in the datasheet) | power.md:360-374, 385-386; decisions.md:34; power_calc_lmr.py:6, 9, 32 (TEDGE), 22-25 (no dropout loss); [LMR] 7.4, 9.2.2.9 ("values in Thermal Information are not valid for design purposes"); [LP38] 6.3 | State the airflow assumption explicitly: full budget only with prop-wash/forced air. Define a no-airflow budget (ground idle / bench, e.g. VTX in pit mode, LED ≤ 0.3 A) and put it in the user notes. Redo 12.2 as a board-level estimate (Pboard × Rboard + ψJB × Pic) including U2 dropout and a 20 ns edge sensitivity case. Make the Gate 1/bring-up thermal measurement (R8) a **pass/fail gate** at 1.8 A + 1.5 A on 2S and 6S. Optionally lower fSW to 400-500 kHz (a smaller switching term; L/C remain within Eq. 11 and Table 9-1) |
| P0-02 | **MAJOR** | **Requirement gap with no accepted deviation: the 10 V rail cannot reach 10 V on 2S or low 3S.** The requirements specify a 2-6S input (6.0-25.2 V) and a 10 V HD-VTX rail. VOUT = **6.2-6.35 V at 7.4 V** and **7.2-7.3 V at 8.4 V** (full 2S), which is below the DJI O3's 7.4 V minimum even on a fully charged 2S pack. power.md 3.4 calls this a "documented limitation" but it is not in decisions.md | requirements.md:19-20; decisions.md D-03 (lines 30-40); power.md:174-179 | The lead must either log a deviation ("10 V rail regulates only for VIN ≥ 11.5 V; 2S HD-VTX unsupported") or add a hardware option: e.g. a solder jumper or DNP 0R from VBAT to the VTX connector/10V pad for 2S builds (with U2 EN held off), or a buck-boost |
| P0-03 | MINOR | **The Eq. 7 "guaranteed" output currents ignore L (±20 %), fSW (−18/+24 %) and spread spectrum (±8 %).** Worst-tolerance recomputation: 5 V **1.87-1.90 A** (2S / 42 V; the doc says 1.96-2.20) against the 1.79 A budget, a margin of only 0.08-0.11 A. 10 V at 25.2 V is limited by HS-limit-min minus ripple to **1.78 A** (1.57 A at the 42 V clamp), so D-03's "~2 A peak" and power.md's "1.97 A guaranteed floor" are not guaranteed. | P0-04 | MINOR | **Dropout model ignores DMAX** (it assumes 100 % duty). The 5 V rail holds regulation only down to **6.25-6.37 V** (doc 5.9 V) and gives 4.76-4.87 V at 6.0 V. ## Verdict

**FAIL**: 2 MAJOR (P0-01 thermal basis, P0-02 10 V rail on 2S/3S with no logged deviation), 8 MINOR, 7 INFO. No BLOCKER: I found no
abs-max violation or back-feed path in the normal states (battery, USB-only, both, VTX off, reset/DFU). Nothing should smoke at first
power-up.
